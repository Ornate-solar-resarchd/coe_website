About the Centre

Vision, mission and messages from the leadership of the Centre of Excellence.

Vision

To be a globally recognized Centre of Excellence in Power Electronics and Control Engineering, advancing world-class technologies and enabling the development of next-generation power electronic products through innovation, research, and engineering excellence.

Mission

To research, design, prototype, and helping an indigenous, reconfigurable power-conversion platform based on a unified control core, enabling applications in solar inverters, Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS), EV fast chargers, microgrid converters, and solid-state transformers, while fostering strong collaboration between academia and industry.

Director’s Message

Bridging academic research and industrial innovation

We are pleased to announce that Ornate Agencies Pvt. Ltd., through its Department of Scientific and Industrial Research (DSIR)-recognized R&D facility, is expanding its activities as a Centre of Excellence in Power Electronics, Control Engineering, and Advanced Energy Systems.

Our vision is to develop innovative hardware and software platforms that can serve as the foundation for a wide range of power engineering products, including solar inverters, battery energy storage systems (BESS), EV charging solutions, microgrid converters, and future smart-grid technologies. We are steadily building the laboratory infrastructure, engineering capabilities, and research frameworks required to design and develop next-generation power-conversion architectures from concept to commercialization.

The Centre is focused on indigenous research and development, with the objective of creating world-class technologies that combine innovation, reliability, and practical industrial application. Our multidisciplinary teams are actively engaged in developing advanced control algorithms, digital power-conversion systems, embedded software platforms, and intelligent energy-management solutions.

We are also committed to fostering strong collaborations with leading academic institutions, research organizations, and industry partners both in India and abroad. Through these partnerships, we aim to accelerate technology development, knowledge exchange, and innovation in the field of power electronics and renewable energy systems.

An important part of our mission is to nurture future engineering talent. The Centre will provide internship, training, and project opportunities for undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral students, enabling them to gain hands-on experience in cutting-edge research and industrial product development.

As we continue this journey, we look forward to contributing to India’s technological advancement and establishing a globally recognized Centre of Excellence that bridges the gap between academic research and industrial innovation.

Dr. Bidyut K. Bhattacharyya Director, Ornate Agencies Pvt. Ltd.
