Facilities

What we have in the lab as equipment — and what we are capable of measuring and doing.

Equipment & Capabilities

Measurement and test infrastructure

The instruments we use to design, characterise and validate power-electronics hardware in-house.

Differential Probe

Keysight N2790A

100 MHz high-voltage differential probe (up to 1400 V) for safe measurement of floating and high-voltage nodes.

Oscilloscope

Keysight InfiniiVision DSOX3014G

100 MHz, 4-channel digital storage oscilloscope — waveform capture for switching and control debug.

Current Probe

Keysight 1146B

AC/DC clamp current probe, 100 kHz / 100 A — non-intrusive current measurement on the oscilloscope.

DC Power Supply

Powertron DC Regulated Supply

Regulated CV/CC DC bench supply for powering and characterising devices under test.

Environmental Chamber

Temperature & Humidity Chamber

Programmable chamber for temperature and humidity conditioning and reliability testing of assemblies.

Soldering

Bakon 60 W Soldering Station

Temperature-controlled digital soldering station for precision assembly of power and control PCBs.

Soldering / Rework

Sugon Soldering Station

Fast-heating soldering station for fine-pitch assembly and component-level rework.

Power Analyzer

Everfine PF9833

Three-phase PWM digital power meter for efficiency, harmonic and power-quality measurement on inverters.

PV Simulator · 18 kVA

ITECH IT6000C — 18 kVA

Bidirectional regenerative DC supply with solar I–V curve simulation (EN50530) for high-power PV-inverter MPPT testing.

PV Simulator · 6 kVA

ITECH IT6000C — 6 kVA

Compact bidirectional PV-array simulator for programmable solar source conditions and string-inverter validation.
